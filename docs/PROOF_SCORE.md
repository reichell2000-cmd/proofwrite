# Proof Score v0.1

Purpose: teacher triage. It summarizes how much observable process evidence exists.

It MUST NOT be presented as:
- probability that the student is the author
- probability of AI use
- probability of cheating

Provisional dimensions:
1. Thought Trace 30
2. My Proof continuity 25
3. Input Evidence 20
4. Revision Evidence 15
5. Process Continuity 10

All weights and thresholds are pilot hypotheses. Calibration must compare false alerts across fast typists, accessibility users, different devices/keyboards, IME behavior, and legitimate paste/citation workflows.

Student UI should prefer qualitative wording ("작성과정이 충분히 기록되었습니다") over a forensic-looking number.
